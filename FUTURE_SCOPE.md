# Future Scope & Roadmap — LexiSight AI

---

## 🚀 Post-Hackathon Vision & Expansion

LexiSight AI has the potential to evolve from a hackathon prototype into a global accessibility standard for **visual cognition adaptation across digital, physical, and immersive reading environments**.

## 🔮 1. Advanced Feature Roadmap

### A. Webcam Eye-Tracking Integration (`WebGazer.js`)
- **Concept**: Eliminate manual mouse tracking by using standard laptop webcams or mobile front cameras to track real-time visual gaze coordinates $(X,Y)$.
- **Feature**: The optical line spotlight dynamically slides down the text lines in sync with the user's actual eye fixation, automatically dimming non-fixated text blocks.

### B. Mobile AR Live Camera Reader (iOS / Android)
- **Concept**: A real-time Augmented Reality overlay camera app (built with Flutter / Unity AR Foundation).
- **Feature**: Users point their smartphone camera at physical book pages, signs, or exam papers. LexiSight overlays micro-anchors and spatial reflow onto the physical paper live on screen.

### C. Multi-Language & Indic Script Support
- **Concept**: Adapt the directional glyph anchor engine to Indian regional languages (e.g., Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali) where complex conjunct consonants and visually similar character glyphs create reading challenges.

---

## 🤖 2. Advanced AI & Machine Learning Enhancements

### A. Reinforcement Learning for Personalized Visual Profiles
- **Concept**: Train a lightweight on-device ML model that passively observes reading speed, saccadic regression rates (backward eye jumps), and pause duration.
- **Feature**: Automatically auto-tunes kerning, line height, and anchor intensity in real time as reading fatigue sets in.

### B. Contextual Visual Synthesizer
- **Concept**: Integrate generative AI models (SD-Turbo / Flux) to create inline real-time visual sketch diagrams for abstract literary metaphors, enhancing whole-concept comprehension.

### C. Automated Dyseidetic Screening & Diagnostic Telemetry
- **Concept**: Develop a 3-minute gamified diagnostic test that measures visual search velocity, mirror letter identification latency, and spatial tracking stability.
- **Feature**: Generates diagnostic reports for parents, teachers, and occupational therapists to detect Dyseidetic Dyslexia early in school-age children.

1. **EdTech & LMS Integration**: Provide an accessibility plugin for platforms like Google Classroom, Canvas, Moodle, and DIKSHA (India National Teacher Platform), enabling visual adaptation for millions of students.
2. **E-Reader & OS Extensions**: Partner with e-reader manufacturers (Kindle, Kobo) to embed LexiSight visual adaptation rules directly into e-ink display firmware.
3. **Assistive Web Accessibility SDK**: Package LexiSight as a 2-line JavaScript SDK that enterprise websites and government portals can embed to meet WCAG 3.0 visual accessibility standards.
